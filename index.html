Dr. Avinash Kumar is a researcher specializing in protein biochemistry, enzymology, nanobiotechnology, and plant seed storage proteins. Currently working as an ANRF-NPDF Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur.

He completed his Ph.D. in Biotechnology (2024) at Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi under the supervision of Prof. Arvind M. Kayastha. His doctoral research focused on the purification and characterization of α-amylase from fenugreek (Trigonella foenum-graecum) seeds.

At IIT Kanpur, in the laboratory of Dr. Ashwani Kumar Thakur, he is investigating the amyloid-like structural organization of seed storage proteins in legumes (chickpea).

Research Areas

Exploring enzyme science from molecule to application

Biochemistry

Enzyme Kinetics

Studying catalytic mechanisms, substrate inhibition, and thermal behavior using classical and non-linear kinetic models.

Kinetics

Enzyme Immobilization

Developing nano-scaffolds (MoSe₂ nanoflowers, functionalized nanomaterials) for enhanced enzyme stability and reusability.

Nanotechnology

Computational Biology

Molecular docking, dynamics simulations, and in silico studies to understand enzyme-ligand interactions at atomic resolution.

Bioinformatics
