👤 About

Researcher & Scientist

Dr. Avinash Kumar is a Project Scientist at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur, working at the intersection of enzyme technology, nanobiotechnology, and computational biology. His research focuses on understanding the molecular behavior of plant-derived enzymes — particularly α-amylase from fenugreek — through spectroscopic, kinetic, and in silico approaches.

With a strong foundation in protein biochemistry and enzyme immobilization on nanomaterials, his work bridges fundamental enzymology with practical applications in food science, pharmaceuticals, and anti-biofilm therapeutics. He has published in high-impact journals including Food Chemistry, Journal of Molecular Structure, and Spectrochimica Acta Part A.

Research Areas

Exploring enzyme science from molecule to application

Biochemistry

Enzyme Kinetics

Studying catalytic mechanisms, substrate inhibition, and thermal behavior using classical and non-linear kinetic models.

Enzyme Immobilization

Nanotechnology

Computational Biology

Bioinformatics
